## Interview Questions (English)

*Semi-structured interview divided into 4 stages following HCI guidelines (Background, Going Deep, Clarifications & Failures, Reflections). Approximately 9 core questions + natural, unbiased follow-ups without leading assumptions.*

### Phase 1: Background & Context (4–5 min)

1. **"To start, would you like to tell me a little about yourself and walk me through what a typical day looks like for you, from morning to evening?"**
   - *Neutral follow-up:* What activities or routines do you most enjoy spending your time on during the week?
2. **"Looking back to when you stopped working, what are the main changes you noticed in your daily pace and habits since retiring?"**

### Phase 2: Going Deep: Relationships and Self-Care (6–7 min)

3. **"Moving on to your interactions with other people: how do your relationships typically take shape during the week, between phone calls, meetups, or time spent on your own?"**
   - *Neutral follow-up:* Are there moments when you prefer being on your own? And moments when you wish for more company? Could you tell us about that?
4. **"Thinking about your daily routines — such as meals, physical activity, or rest — how do you usually organize yourself throughout the week?"**
   - *Follow-up (only if appointments or therapies are mentioned):* How do you organize yourself to keep track of schedules or deadlines? Do you use reminders, or do you rely on memory?

### Phase 3: Obstacles, Unforeseen Events, and Tools (Clarifications & Failures) (5–6 min)

5. **"If an off-day, a worry, or a minor practical inconvenience comes up, what do you usually do to deal with it? Who or what do you turn to?"**
   - *Follow-up:* Does a recent situation come to mind where you had to resolve a small unexpected issue like that?
6. **"On the other hand, when good news or a particularly pleasant moment happens during the week, what do you like to do, or who do you enjoy sharing it with?"**
7. **"Could you tell me about the last time you used a phone, computer, or an app to get something practical done or look up information (e.g., local services, schedules, events)? How did it go?"**
   - *Follow-up (if friction arises):* Was there any part of the process that was unclear or got you stuck? How did you manage to resolve it?

### Phase 4: Reflections & Wrap-up (3–4 min)

8. **"Reflecting on your overall experience as a retiree, what aspects of your current life do you find most rewarding, and which ones, if any, do you find most demanding?"**
9. **"Is there anything else about your daily life or well-being that we haven't touched on, but that you think is important for us to know?"**
   - *Formal thank you and closing.*
