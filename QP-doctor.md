## Phase 1: Background and professional lens (4-5 min)
1. "Could you tell me about your work with older adults and what a typical week looks like for you?"
2. "When you think about wellbeing in later life, what does it mean to you in your professional experience? Is it something you think about in a particular way, or not really?"
   - *Follow-up:* "Do patients describe it in similar terms, or differently, or do they not really talk about it??"

## Phase 2: Going deep: what the doctor observes (8-10 min)
3. "When an older person comes in for a visit, is there anything in particular you pay attention to(if any)?"
4. "Among the patients you see, do you notice differences between those who are doing well and those who are not?"
   - *Follow-up if there is a differencce:* "What do you think explains that difference?"
5. "Do you notice any patterns in how people’s lives change after they stop working, or nothing in particular"
   - *Follow-up:* "Does that vary from person to person or no?"
6. "What do you notice about how patients talk about their mobility, memory, sleep, eating, or mood?"
   - *Follow-up:* "Which of these do patients bring up themselves, and which do you have to ask about?"
7. "Do family, friends, or caregivers play a role in your patients' health and daily life?"
   - *Follow-up:* "Can you describe a case where this made a noticeable difference, in either direction?"
8. "How do medications and treatment plans fit into your patients' everyday routines?"
   - *Follow-up:* "What tends to help or get in the way of following them? Or nothing in particular?"

## Phase 3: System, prevention, and tools (6-8 min)
9. "What support or services are available to older adults in your area(if any), and how do patients find out about them?"
   - *Follow-up:* "What have you noticed about who uses them and who doesn't?"
10. "What do you typically do in your practice to look after older patients’ health over time?"
    - *Follow-up:* "How do patients respond to them?"
11. "How, if at all, do digital tools come into your work with older patients?
    - *Follow-up:* "Can you think of a specific example of how that went?"
12. "Has a patient’s wellbeing ever depended on something that was hard to arrange, or has that not come up in your experience?"

## Phase 4: Reflections (3-4 min)
13. "When researchers or designers work on older adults’ wellbeing, what do you think they tend to focus on, and what gets less attention, or are they covered well?"
14. "Is there anything we haven't discussed that you think would be important for us to understand or no?"
